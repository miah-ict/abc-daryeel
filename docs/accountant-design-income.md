# Accountant income: screen and interaction spec

Use the existing accountant shell, Manrope/DM Sans type, pale blue canvas, white panels, and restrained blue, mint, and amber status colors described in `docs/accountant-design-shell.md`. Under **Income**, expose four destinations in this order: **Student Fees**, **Fee Collection**, **Outstanding Fees**, **Other Income**. Keep the current invoice and student account views as linked detail destinations. Page headings and primary actions should use plain English, with short Somali helper text where it helps the operator; do not rely on translation alone for a control's meaning.

## Student Fees

**List screen.** A compact summary row shows active plans, charges due this month, collected against those charges, and remaining balance, each with its period and currency. Below it, place search plus academic period, class, and status filters above a table of plan/charge name, assignment, line-item total, due date, period, status, and actions. A class plan card can still show tuition, learning materials, and transport, but a separate student charge or issued invoice must be visible in account history. Primary action: **Create fee plan / charge**; row action: **View details** or **Edit** while editable. Empty state: “No fee plans or charges for these filters” with **Create fee plan**.

**Create/edit drawer.** First choose **Fee plan** (reusable schedule) or **Student charge** (specific receivable). Request title, academic period, due date, one or more line items with nonnegative amounts, and optional class/student assignment. Show a running total and a review summary before saving. For a charge, show the assigned student/class clearly and prevent an accidental duplicate for the same student, period, and purpose. Explain beside **Save**: “Editing this plan will not change invoices already issued.” Helper labels: **Academic period — Xilli dugsiyeed**, **Due date — Taariikhda kama dambaysta ah**, **Amount — Lacagta**. On success, open the saved detail or return to the filtered list with a status message.

**Student account detail.** From a fee, invoice, or Students row, show student identity, guardian, charges/invoices, paid amount, refunded amount, current balance, and a dated history of collections and adjustments. Keep the **Collect payment** action beside the current balance; hide or disable it when there is no collectible balance and explain why.

## Fee Collection

**Start screen.** Search by student name/ID or invoice/charge number; show only valid open charges for the accountant's school. Selecting one displays student, class, period, charge total, prior payments/refunds, due date, status, and **remaining balance** in a fixed summary above the form. If the student has multiple open items, require an explicit selection. Preselect the outstanding amount but permit a partial amount. Helper: **Remaining balance — Haraaga harsan**.

**Collect form.** Require amount, payment date, method, and the selected charge/invoice. Add payer name and external reference fields where relevant, with optional notes. Inline validation rejects zero/negative amounts, more than the current balance, invalid dates, and missing required details; display the valid amount range beside the field. A short review step repeats student, target charge, amount, method, and resulting balance. The final button reads **Record payment and issue receipt** (**Diiwaangeli lacagta oo bixi rasiid**). Disable it while posting and prevent repeat submission. If the server reports a changed balance, refresh the summary and ask the accountant to review the new amount. Never imply that recording a cash/bank/mobile payment transfers money.

**Success and receipt.** On confirmed posting, replace the form with a clear **Payment recorded** state showing server-issued receipt number, payment/ledger reference, amount, date, method, student, applied charge, and new balance. Make **Print receipt**, **Download receipt**, **View student account**, and **Collect another** visible actions. The receipt uses the school name/details and payer, and remains findable in the Receipts register. A failed post shows a persistent error in the form; no receipt preview or success toast should appear until the posted payment and receipt are confirmed. A retry after an uncertain network response must resolve the original submission rather than create another collection.

## Outstanding Fees

Show a total outstanding amount and count with an explicit **as of** date. Use search, class, due-date range, status (**Pending**, **Partial**, **Overdue**, **Paid**), and aging filters (**Not due**, **1–30**, **31–60**, **61+ days**). The table has student and class, invoice/charge ID, original charge, paid, current balance, due date, age, status, and **Collect** action. Sort overdue items by oldest due date by default; totals and export must reflect the stated filter scope. Paid rows appear only when selected by status and have **View history** instead of **Collect**. A balance of zero reads **Paid** even if its original due date has passed. Include an empty result message that names the active filters and offers **Clear filters**. Helper labels: **Outstanding fees — Lacagaha harsan**, **Overdue — Waqtigii dhaafay**.

## Other Income

A ledger list shows date, source/payer, category, method, amount, reference, receipt number, and status, with search, date/category/method filters, and an export matching the visible scope. Primary action: **Record other income**. The form requires source, category, date, method, and positive amount; reference and notes are available with a clear optional label. Review the posting details before **Record income and issue receipt**. A confirmed post creates one income ledger entry and opens the same receipt success panel as a fee collection. Keep school fees out of this free-form path when they should be applied to a student's charge; route the accountant to **Fee Collection**. Helper labels: **Source — Isha dakhliga**, **Reference — Tixraac**, **Notes — Faahfaahin**.

## Shared states and responsive behavior

- Reserve stable space for loading skeletons in summaries and tables. Show inline retry for load failures and preserve entered form data after a save failure. Distinguish “no records yet” from “no matches.” Confirm a completed post with the returned amount and receipt, not a generic toast.
- Keep monetary values right aligned with tabular numerals, USD formatting consistent with the workspace, and the applied date range visible next to every total. Status text accompanies color. An invoice/charge contributes to outstanding balance when issued; income totals change only after a payment posts. Refunds update the displayed balance and history through the server's returned values.
- Above 700 px, use the existing panel/table style; put the transaction summary beside the collection form when space allows. On narrow screens, stack the summary above the form, turn dense rows into labeled cards or contain horizontal scrolling inside the table panel, keep filters in an expandable group, and keep the primary action reachable without covering fields. Targets are at least 44 px high.
- Every field has a persistent label and any Somali helper as secondary text. Connect errors and hints to inputs, announce post success/error in a live region, move focus to the first invalid field, and return focus to the triggering action after a drawer closes. Dialogs trap focus, close on Escape when safe, and do not discard an in-progress post. Receipt actions have descriptive accessible names; printed receipts remain legible in monochrome.
